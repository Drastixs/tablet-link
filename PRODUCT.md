# Tablet Link

## Purpose

Turn a personal Android tablet into an extended Linux display, microphone, and speaker over a private Tailscale network. Opening the tablet app connects to one saved desktop automatically.

## Users

One owner with a Pixel tablet and a Niri/Wayland Linux desktop. Initial setup may require administrator access; daily use should not.

## Experience

The tablet display sits to the left of the main display. A single connection carries video, touch (tap, drag, scroll), microphone, and speaker audio. Leaving the app disconnects immediately. Failure of any part disconnects all parts and shows a clear reason.

## Register

product

## Tone

Quiet, direct, and practical. Show connection state and actionable failures without technical jargon during normal use.
